### 2.0 Case Study on Virtual Reality (VR)
Virtual reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that users can interact with in a realistic way. It uses specialized equipment like head-mounted displays to immerse the user by replacing their view of the real world with a synthetic one, engaging senses like sight and hearing to create a feeling of "presence". In a Virtual Reality (VR) experience, users are fully immersed in a simulated digital environment. Individuals must put on a VR headset or head-mounted display to get a 360-degree view of an artificial world that fools their brain into believing they are, e.g., walking on the moon, swimming under the ocean or stepped into whatever new world the VR developers created.

The gaming and entertainment industry were early adopters of this technology; however, companies in several industries such as healthcare, construction, engineering, the military, and more are finding VR to be very useful. VR is an immensely important category of new and maturing technologies that will play a central role for the human experience in the near and further future. For more details, please visit: https://www.viverse.com/events/hackathon and https://secondlife.com/.

### Project Requirements
Do your desktop research and answer the following THREE (3) questions:

1. Do an analysis on existing solution for VR applications in Malaysia and/or globally from the aspects of the services and functionalities provided by the solution. Then, propose FIVE (5) possible mobile/web solution/features/functions for VR applications in Malaysia and/or globally. (10 marks)
2. To develop the mobile app/web app/hybrid app prototype on VR. High fidelity alpha version prototype is required with mock-ups of the user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) of your FIVE (5) innovative solution/features/functions in question 1, and include major functionalities. You need to have minimum FIVE (5) UI-UX screens for your app prototype. (10 marks)
3. Justify and explain what UI-UX development methodology you are planning to use, e.g. User-Centered Design (UCD), Design Thinking by Stanford University, Design Thinking and Innovation at Apple, etc. (10 marks)
